Serpico is Farnese's servant and personal bodyguard. He is the illegitimate son of the noble Federico III of the Vandimion family who joins Guts' traveling party along with his half-sister Farnese.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Farnese de Vandimion, Guts's traveling party, Tower of Conviction, Albion, Guts and Berserker Armor. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
