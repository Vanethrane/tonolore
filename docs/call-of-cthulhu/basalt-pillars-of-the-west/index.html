The Basalt Pillars of the West are fictional structures in the Cthulhu Mythos. They were originally created by the late American author H. P. Lovecraft for his short story "The White Ship".

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Call of Cthulhu, Nathaniel Elton, H. P. Lovecraft, Cthulhu Mythos, Dreamlands and Cathuria. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
