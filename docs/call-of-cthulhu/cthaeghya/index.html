Cthaeghya is a fictional entity created by Kevin L. O'Brien for his short story "October Surprise", depicting the character as the sister of Cthulhu and responsible for spawning the Cthulhi.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Call of Cthulhu, Ghatanothoa, Klosmiebhyx, Elder Sign, Outer Gods and Ghizguth. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
