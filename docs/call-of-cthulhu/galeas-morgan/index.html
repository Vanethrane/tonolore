Officer Galeas Morgan is a character created by Fantasy Flight Games for their Arkham Horror Fiction series of Cthulhu Mythos stories. He made his first appearance in the 2017 novella Hour of the Huntress by Dave Gross.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Call of Cthulhu, Jenny Barnes, Miskatonic University, Cthulhu Mythos, Shub-Niggurath and Outer Gods. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
