Ilarnek is a fictional location in the Cthulhu Mythos. It was originally created by the late American author H. P. Lovecraft for his short story "The Doom That Came to Sarnath".

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Call of Cthulhu, Falona, H. P. Lovecraft, Cthulhu Mythos, Dreamlands and Kadatheron. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
