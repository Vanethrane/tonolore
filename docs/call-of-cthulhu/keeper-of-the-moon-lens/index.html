The Keeper of the Moon-Lens is an avatar of Shub-Niggurath created by Ramsey Campbell for his short story "The Moon-Lens".

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Call of Cthulhu, Great Old Ones, Shub-Niggurath, Magna Mater, Keeper and Cthulhu Mythos. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
