Mlandoth is a fictional entity in the expanded Cthulhu Mythos. The character was originally created by Walter C. DeBill, Jr. for his short story "Where Yidhra Walks".

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Call of Cthulhu, Cthulhu Mythos, Mril Thorion, Ngyr-Khorath, Outer Gods and Azathoth. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
