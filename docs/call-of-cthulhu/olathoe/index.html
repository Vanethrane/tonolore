Olathoë is a fictional extra-dimensional location in the Cthulhu Mythos. It was originally created by the late American author H. P. Lovecraft for his short story "Polaris".

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Call of Cthulhu, Daikos, Mount Kadiphonek, Mount Noton, Plateau of Sarkis and H. P. Lovecraft. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
