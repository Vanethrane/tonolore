Sona-Nyl, also known as the Land of Fancy, is a fictional location in the Cthulhu Mythos. It was originally created by the late American author H. P. Lovecraft for his short story "The White Ship".

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Call of Cthulhu, H. P. Lovecraft, Cthulhu Mythos, Dreamlands, Cathuria and Cthulhu. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
