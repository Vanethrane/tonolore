S'tya-Yg'Nalle (The Whiteness) is a fictional entity created by James Ambuehl and E. P. Berglund for their 1997 short story "Whiteout".

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Call of Cthulhu, Great Old Ones, Nyarlathotep, Dimension, Ithaqua and Avatar. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
