Taranushi (also known as Lord of the Jinn) is a jinn from North African mythology. It was later incorporated into the Call of Cthulhu role-playing game by Chaosium. (: Cthulhu Invictus)

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Call of Cthulhu, Outer Gods, Dimension, Chaosium, Cthulhu and Jinn. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
