Tsathoggua, also known as the Sleeper of N'kai, is a fictional entity in the Cthulhu Mythos. The character was originally created by Clark Ashton Smith for his Hyperborean Cycle.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Call of Cthulhu, Cthulhu Mythos, Abholos, Atlach-Nacha, Ghizguth and Gol-goroth. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
