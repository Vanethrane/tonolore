Kaburamaru is a medium-sized albino Japanese rat snake with reddish pink eyes and large fangs on either side of his mouth. His scales and underbelly are both white due to albinism.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Demon Slayer, Sanemi Shinazugawa, Shinjuro Rengoku, Mitsuri Kanroji, Tanjiro Kamado and Kyojuro Rengoku. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
