Susamaru was a pale-skinned woman with bright yellow eyes, cat-like slits, and white horizontal slats. She had black hair that transitioned to orange at the tips, styled with short, spiky bangs at the front.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Demon Slayer, Muzan Kibutsuji, Tanjiro Kamado, Tamayo, Yahaba and Asakusa. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
