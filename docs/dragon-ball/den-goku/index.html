Den-Goku is seen to equally resemble both his original counterparts: Dende and Goku. Den-Goku has Dende's Namekian body and Goku's hairstyle. For attire, he has Goku's gi and Dende's brown jacket.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Dragon Ball, Instant Transmission, Dragon Ball Z, Dragon Balls, Power level and Saiyans. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
