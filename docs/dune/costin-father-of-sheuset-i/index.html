Count Costin was a man from Salusa Secundus, the son of Count Palaigo Corrino. He was the father of Sheuset I Costin I, who later became the first Emperor of the Known Universe and founded House Corrino.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Dune, Audrii III, Basil III at-Tam, Corrin V, House Harkonnen and Salusa Secundus. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
