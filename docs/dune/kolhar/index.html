Kolhar was a cold and largely barren planet located near the Rossak system, and on the nearby trade routes from Ginaz. Despite being uninhabited, it's environment was viable by humans.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Dune, Spacing Guild, Planet, Rossak and Ginaz. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
