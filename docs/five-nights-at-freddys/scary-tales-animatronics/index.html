The Scary Tales animatronics are animatronic skin sets that appear in Five Nights at Freddy's AR: Special Delivery and became available for the Scary Tales Event on April 19, 2021.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Five Nights at Freddy's, Classic Animatronics, Endoskeleton, Freddy Fazbear's Pizza, Freddy Fazbear and Chica. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
