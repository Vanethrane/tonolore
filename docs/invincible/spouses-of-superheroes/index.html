The Spouses of Superheroes, or SOS for short, is a therapy community group to help nurture widowed spouses of superheroes who were killed in their duty to help others.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Invincible, Murder of the Guardians of the Globe, Guardians of the Globe, Debbie Grayson, Green Ghost II and Omni-Man. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
