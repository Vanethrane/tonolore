Fukashigi Nijimura (虹村ふかしぎ - Niji-mura fukashigi) is an ally in the light novel JORGE JOESTAR from the 37th universe. He is a Stand User who wields the Stand NYPD Blue, and is the twin brother of Muryotaisu Nijimura.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, Muryotaisu Nijimura, Okuyasu Nijimura, Yoshikage Kira, Joji Joestar and Koji Hirose. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
