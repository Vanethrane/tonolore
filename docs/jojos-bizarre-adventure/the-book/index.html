The Book appears as a hard covered tankoubon-size book with a brown leather cover on which traces of ancient scratches can be seen. Its cape has no title or author name. It is about 380 pages thick, about 3 centimeters.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, Morioh, Crybaby Boy, Hanae Orikasa, The Scribe Ani and Takuma Hasumi. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
