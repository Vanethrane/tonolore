Journey to the West (西遊記) is a Chinese novel published in the 16th century during the Ming dynasty and attributed to Wu Cheng'en. It is one of the Four Great Classical Novels of Chinese literature.

Dipankara Dipankara (Pali: Dīpaṅkara; Sanskrit: Dīpaṃkara, "Lamp bearer") or Dipankara Buddha is one of the Buddhas of the past. He is said to have lived on Earth four asankheyyas and one hundred thousand kalpas ago. According to… Golden Cicada The Golden Cicada was an original disciple of the Tathagata Buddha, but was unable to escape the cycle of reincarnation. He was reincarnated as Tang Sanzang. Bull Scholar The Bull Scholar is a hunchback that walks with a slumbering gait. He wore a calm green robe and a cap with two horns.
