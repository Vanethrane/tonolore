Blaze is a young female Atrociraptor that first appeared in Jurassic World: Chaos Theory. She was one of the seven Atrociraptors created for Project Theropod Axis by Biosyn in their Sanctuary.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Jurassic Park, Jurassic World, Atrociraptor, Velociraptor and Chaos. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
