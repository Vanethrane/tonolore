Dollar Bill is a henchman of the evil Dr. Inferno. He appears in only one set, 8968 River Heist. He carries a golden gun and works alongside Dyna-Mite.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to LEGO, Inferno Henchman, Minifigures, Dr. Inferno, Agent Fuse and Gold Tooth. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
