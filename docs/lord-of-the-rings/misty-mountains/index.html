The Misty Mountains, also named Hithaeglir, was a mountain range stretching 1280 kilometres (796 miles) from Mount Gundabad in the far north to Methedras in the south of Middle-earth, between Eriador and Rhovanion.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to The Lord of the Rings, Gollum, Gimli, Elrond, Rivendell and Lothlórien. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
