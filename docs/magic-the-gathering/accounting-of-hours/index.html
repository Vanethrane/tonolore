The Accounting of Hours was the primary religious text of the Amonkheti during Nicol Bolas's reign as the plane's God-Pharaoh. It is made up of a series of prophecies featured in the flavor text of the Amonkhet block.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Magic: The Gathering, Record of Days, Nicol Bolas, Second Sun, Phyrexians and Amonkhet. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
