Esper is one of five Shards of Alara. It is primarily blue-aligned, with white and black as secondary colors. After the Conflux, Upper Vectis on Esper is located south along the coast from Valeron.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Magic: The Gathering, Baltrice, Crucius, Grinth, Infinity Globe and Alara. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
