Sarkhan Vol is a human former planeswalker hailing from Tarkir, a plane scourged by war. He is fascinated by dragons, something that has been a blessing and curse to him throughout his life.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Magic: The Gathering, Planeswalkers, Planeswalker, Tarkir, Spirit and Nicol Bolas. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
