Yoshi (full name: T. Yoshisaur Munchakoopas) is a dinosaur-like character who acts as an ally of Mario and Luigi and is the protagonist of his own franchise. He first appears in Super Mario World.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Mario, Mario, Koopa Troop, Luigi, Princess Peach and Mario Kart. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
