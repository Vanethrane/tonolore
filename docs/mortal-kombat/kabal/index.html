Kabal is a character in the Mortal Kombat fighting game series. A member of the Black Dragon clan with superhuman speed, Kabal has been a recurring antagonist while remaining one of series' most popular characters.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Mortal Kombat, Kira, Kobra, Doctor, Mortal Kombat 11 and Mortal Kombat 3. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
