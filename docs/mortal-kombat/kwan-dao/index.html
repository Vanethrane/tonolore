The Kwan Dao (關刀) is a type of Chinese pole weapon. In Chinese, it is properly called a "crescent moon blade" (偃月刀).

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Mortal Kombat, Mortal Kombat: Armageddon, Mortal Kombat: Deception, Mortal Kombat: Unchained, Mortal Kombat: Conquest and Mortal Kombat 11. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
