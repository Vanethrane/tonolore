Madam Bo (波夫人) is a minor character in the Mortal Kombat fighting game series. The owner of the Fengjian Teahouse, Bo is a former associate of the Lin Kuei and the mother of Sektor in the current timeline.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Mortal Kombat, Bo' Rai Cho, Earthrealm, Kung Lao, Liu Kang and Lin Kuei. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
