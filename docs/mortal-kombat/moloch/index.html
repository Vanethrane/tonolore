Moloch is a character in the Mortal Kombat fighting game series. He made his debut in Mortal Kombat: Deadly Alliance, in which he also served as the sub-boss. Moloch then became playable in Mortal Kombat: Armageddon.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Mortal Kombat, Mortal Kombat: Deadly Alliance, Mortal Kombat: Armageddon, Mystical Orb, Shang Tsung and Netherrealm. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
