The Nexus is a plane of existence that exists as the central hub between the various realms of existence. The concept was first introduced in the game Mortal Kombat: Deception, as part of the game's Konquest mode.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Mortal Kombat, Damashi, Monster, Mortal Kombat: Deception, Amulet of Shinnok and Netherrealm. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
