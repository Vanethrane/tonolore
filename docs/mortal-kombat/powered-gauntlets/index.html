Powered Gauntlets are weaponized gauntlets used by Jacqui Briggs in Mortal Kombat X and Mortal Kombat 11. The way they increase her strength is most likely to emulate Jacqui's father, Jax.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Mortal Kombat, Gauntlets, Mortal Kombat 11, Mortal Kombat X, Jacqui Briggs and Bionic Arms. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
