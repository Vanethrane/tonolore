Zenkaro is a minor character that appeared in Mortal Kombat: Defenders of the Realm. He is a colonel of Shao Kahn's Shokan forces that only appeared in episode 7, "Fall From Grace".

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Mortal Kombat, Mortal Kombat: Defenders of the Realm, Kurtis Stryker, Sonya Blade, Earthrealm and Shao Kahn. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
