8-bit and Gaffer are two male ponies, 8-bit a Pegasus and Gaffer a unicorn, who appear in the IDW comics. They are Shining Armor's friends, others being Gizmo and, later, Cherry Fizzy and Spearhead.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to My Little Pony, Princess Cadance, Shining Armor, Cherry Fizzy, Cloud Chaser and Sunburst. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
