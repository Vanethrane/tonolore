Apple Stars is a female unicorn pony with a purple coat, green mane and tail, magenta eyes, and a cutie mark of two green apples. She is unnamed in the show, but she is named in merchandise and other media.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to My Little Pony, Strawberry Sunrise, Princess Erroria, Blueberry Curls, Sunshine Petals and Golden Harvest. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
