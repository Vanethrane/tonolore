Canter Zoom is a male human who appears in the Equestria Girls film Legend of Everfree and the half-hour specials Movie Magic and Mirror Magic. He is a filmmaking director and Juniper Montage's uncle.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to My Little Pony, Chestnut Magnifico, Twilight Sparkle, Juniper Montage, The Rainbooms and Timber Spruce. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
