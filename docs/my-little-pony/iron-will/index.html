Iron Will is a male minotaur and supporting character who first appears in the season two episode Putting Your Hoof Down. He travels around Equestria holding assertiveness seminars with a group of goat assistants.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to My Little Pony, Flim and Flam, Lightning Dust, Pinkie Pie, Fluttershy and Fluttershy. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
