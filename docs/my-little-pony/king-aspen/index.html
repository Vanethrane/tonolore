King Aspen is a male deer who first appears as a supporting character in the IDW comics story The Root of the Problem. He is the ruler of the Kingdom of Thicket in the Everfree Forest.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to My Little Pony, Princess Celestia, Twilight Sparkle, Tempest Shadow, Princess Luna and Queen Novo. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
