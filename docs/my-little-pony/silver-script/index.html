Silver Script is a male background Pegasus pony with a purple coat, gray mane and tail, bluish gray eyes, and a cutie mark of a scroll. He is unnamed in the series, but he is named in Gameloft's mobile game.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to My Little Pony, Apple Cinnamon, Comet Tail, Crusoe Palm, Eiffel and Emerald Green. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
