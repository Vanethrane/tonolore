Sweet Pop is the placeholder name of a Pegasus pony filly who first appears in the season episode The Cutie Mark Chronicles. She has a pale violet coat, two-tone gray mane and tail, and spring green eyes.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to My Little Pony, Aura, Cotton Cloudy, Dinky Doo, Lemony Gem and Liza Doolots. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
