Jako was a self-admitting "rotten person". He is a very cruel man, as he told Sakura Haruno that he would kill her, along with Naruto and Sasuke, as soon as he got his treasure back.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Naruto, Sakura Haruno, Chakra, Naruto Uzumaki and Sasuke Uchiha. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
