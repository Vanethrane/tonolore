Whole Cake Island is a New World island under the control of Big Mom, formerly one of the Four Emperors. It is her crew's base of operations and the central island in Big Mom's 35-island archipelago known as Totto Land.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Whole Cake Island Conflict, Charlotte Linlin, Grand Line, Four Emperors, Totto Land and New World. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
