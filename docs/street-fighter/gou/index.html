Gou (號 ゴウ) is a character originating as concept art for Captain Commando before making his official debut in Street Fighter 6. Trained in Bushinryu Ninjutsu, Gou is Guy's pupil and the father of Ginzu the Ninja.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Street Fighter, Captain Commando, Ginzu the Ninja, Kimberly and Zeku. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
