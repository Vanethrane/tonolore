Mr. de Rochefort is a minor character in the Tekken series. He is a member of the De Rochefort Family, Lili's father, and a wealthy oil magnate living in Monaco. He is the founder of Rochefort Enterprises.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Tekken, Emilie de Rochefort, Jin Kazama, Ganryu, King and Sebastian. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
