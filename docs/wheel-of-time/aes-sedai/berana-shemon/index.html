Berana Shemon is an Aes Sedai of the White Ajah. She has allied herself with the Salidar Aes Sedai and was elected Sitter in the Rebel Hall of the Tower, despite being too young. She has no Warder.

Key threads from this canonical page lead to Aes Sedai, Hall of the Tower, One Power, Shawl and Ajah. Each link is another long-tail path through the subject.
